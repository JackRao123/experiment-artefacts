# Full GLM-5.3 CP8EP1 FSDP: observed communication overlap

Analyzed all eight saved runtime traces from the persistent-buffer BF16 run.
No new training run, model change, or test change. Generated by
`fsdp_overlap.py`; machine-readable results and extracted intervals are under
`cp8ep1/result/analysis/overlap/`.

## Answer

**Important follow-up:** the percentage below measures simultaneous GPU kernel
activity, not whether prefetched weights arrive late. The subsequent deadline
check in `PREFETCH.md` finds that all 75 forward and 74 steady-state backward
expert gathers on every rank finish before the preceding block's GPU work
ends. Thus the 27% without concurrent compute must not be described as 27%
exposed expert-weight communication overhead.

Expert-weight all-gathers overlap non-communication CUDA kernels for about
73% of their observed duration (70.49–76.13% across ranks). This is meaningful
temporal overlap, but is not itself a measurement of latency hiding.

Rank 0, one profiled step:

| Weight communication | Interval-union duration | Concurrent with non-NCCL kernels | Without non-NCCL kernels | Overlap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Expert weights | 4.450 s | 3.248 s | 1.202 s | 72.99% |
| Other model weights | 1.544 s | 0.044 s | 1.501 s | 2.82% |
| All FSDP weight gathers | 5.936 s | 3.261 s | 2.676 s | 54.92% |

The combined row uses interval unions, so it does not equal the arithmetic
sum of the two preceding rows. Some weight collectives overlap each other.
Including GPU memcpy/memset activities as overlap changes rank 0's combined
non-overlapped duration only from 2.676 s to 2.669 s.

Across ranks, expert gathers average 4.371 s of interval-union time, of which
3.187 s overlaps non-NCCL kernels and 1.185 s does not. These are averages
over ranks of the same capture, not averages over independent controls.

## Initial rank-arrival skew matters

The first non-expert weight gather is not representative of steady-state
transfer performance. Rank 0's kernel begins at 6061442.577421 s and ends at
6061443.690542 s: 1.113121 s total, without concurrent compute. Rank 7 enters
at 6061443.689401 s and finishes at 6061443.690554 s: only 1.153 ms. The end
timestamps agree within tens of microseconds across ranks.

This is evidence that early ranks spend most of this first collective waiting
for the last rank to arrive, not transferring weights for a second. The trace
does not by itself establish what caused the late arrival or whether it occurs
in unprofiled controls.

Excluding the first gather explicitly, rank 0's remaining FSDP weight-gather
intervals total 4.823 s: 3.261 s overlapped and 1.563 s without compute,
or **67.60% overlap**. Across ranks this figure ranges from 66.14% to 73.79%.
The initial wait remains included in the main table; it is not silently removed.

## Definitions and limits

- Collective ownership is joined from `record_param_comms` process-group
  descriptions using Kineto External id, not guessed from an NCCL kernel name.
- Compute means the union of all non-NCCL CUDA kernel intervals on the same
  rank. It includes elementwise, permutation, and device copy kernels, not
  just Tensor Core work. The separate `overlap_gemm_s` field uses a narrower
  GEMM-name signature; for rank 0 expert gathers it is 1.461 s.
- Overlap is the intersection of communication and compute interval unions
  across all GPU streams. Nested CPU/GPU annotations are not summed.
- No-concurrent-compute time is **not a proven removable step-time cost**:
  other dependencies or CP communication may be concurrent, and NCCL kernels
  include peer waiting. Conversely, overlapped communication can still slow
  compute through resource contention.
- The captured HTTP forward/backward took 13.833 s, versus 11.714 s mean for
  the unprofiled controls. Rank 0's full GPU activity span, including optimizer
  and surrounding operations, is 14.326 s. Do not divide captured communication
  time by the control time and present the ratio as measured control overhead.
- The 278 CP collective kernels on rank 0 total 1.006 s and do not overlap
  non-NCCL kernels in this capture. That includes a 0.408-s scalar all-reduce
  wait; it is not all bulk activation transfer.
- Import warnings concern overlapping complete-event annotations moved onto
  overflow tracks. Analysis uses unique kernel slice IDs and rejects duplicate
  joins or unattributed NCCL kernels. Interval results satisfy an independent
  union/intersection identity. Input trace SHA256 values are retained.
