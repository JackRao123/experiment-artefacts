The step, per GPU

GPU kernel time by what the kernel is, one bar per GPU. The eight GPUs agree to within a second on everything except the wait time, which moves between them. Hover a segment for the number.

What to do, in order

Ordered by expected gain per unit of effort, not by bucket size. Bars show the ceiling on step time recoverable; the text says how much of the ceiling is realistic.

1

Stop rebuilding the DSA context-parallel layout in every attention call

45 boolean-mask reads (x[mask] → nonzero → host sync) per attention call, 78 layers, two passes: 7,020 of the 7,521 host syncs per step. The inputs are cu_seqlens, identical for all layers. Compute once per microbatch, or on the CPU. confidence higheffort lowgain 5–8%

ceiling ≈ 2.0 s of 27.4 (GPU idle + host-stall share of waits)
2

Expert load imbalance across the 8 EP ranks

Ranks wait 3.9 s per step at HybridEP collectives. Per collective, the last rank to arrive had a busy GPU 90% of the time: it had more expert work, not a stalled host. Heaviest rank does 1.6× the mean expert GEMM per MoE layer (p90 2.0×, max 2.5×). Measured on random tokens: re-measure on real data before investing. confidence higheffort highgain up to 14%

ceiling 3.9 s of 27.4
3

Move the frozen LM head off FP32 CUDA cores

The chunked output head runs as cutlass3x_sm100_simt_sgemm at ~66 TFLOP/s: 16 chunks × ~116 ms = 1.85 s per step. A tensor-core path (bf16 in, fp32 accumulate, upcast the logits) is ≥8× faster. confidence higheffort lowgain ≈6%

ceiling 1.6 s of 27.4
4

Recompute less

The recomputed forward is 6.2 s of GPU time per step (the layer:n ranges that sit inside the backward phase). Peak allocated memory was 213 of 287 GB. Selective recompute trades headroom for part of this; the gain depends on the memory plan, so no number is claimed. confidence high on costeffort medium

ceiling 6.2 s of 27.4
5

DSA backward

FusedSparseAttentionFuncBackward is 4.9 s per step: 3.7 s in one cuDNN kernel (44–56 ms per layer, 5.9× the forward kernel) plus 1.2 s of compaction and copies, plus one host nonzero per layer that waits up to 437 ms. Vendor kernel; check the cuDNN version, remove the compaction round trip. confidence low on a quick win

ceiling unclear; 4.9 s bucket
6

Copies, cats and unfused elementwise

5.1 s per step: 1.7 s of copies plus 0.8 s elementwise inside the true layer backward, 0.8 s of layout cats in the attention projections, SiLU and its gate multiply as separate kernels in the experts (TE has a fused SwiGLU). Many 1–3% items, no single fix. effort medium each

5.1 s bucket, partially recoverable

Host blocked time is not the cost by itself: much of it is the CPU waiting for GPU work that was queued earlier. The cost is what the GPU does after the queue empties. Measured directly: 1.4 to 1.9 s per GPU with no kernel resident (5–7%), plus the host-stall share of collective lateness below.

Why ranks wait at HybridEP collectives

The k-th device_sync_kernel on every rank is the same collective. For each of the 900, take the last rank to arrive and ask whether its GPU had been idle (its host stalled) or busy (it had more work) since the previous collective.

Method and limits

Capture: nsys launch with CUDA, NVTX, cuBLAS, cuDNN and OS-runtime tracing, autograd NVTX, --cudabacktrace=sync:1000 --python-backtrace=cuda; one untraced warmup and control step; then nsys start with CPU sampling and 10 kHz GPU hardware metrics on all 8 devices around exactly one /forward_backward + /optim_step. Exported to sqlite and processed by five scripts in the run folder (REPRODUCING.md lists the commands).

A kernel belongs to the NVTX range that contained its launch on the host; phase and per-layer ranges never overlap within a process, so that is exact. GPU idle is time with no kernel resident. Category totals are means over the 8 GPUs.

Limits: one step, one sequence, synthetic random tokens (finding 2 most affected), about 4% profiler overhead, Python frames unresolved in the call stacks (the libtorch frames plus the enclosing NVTX range placed every site). The instrumentation itself costs under 1.5%: control step 26.7 s with it versus 26.3 s without, inside step-to-step noise.
